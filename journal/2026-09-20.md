## Audio design review after v0.10

User asked whether this is the right approach. Re-read implementation and official
AudioAttributes definitions (https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/AudioAttributes).
Assessment: valid explicit personal alarm-volume choice, but not a universal
Android recommendation. Navigation guidance better describes ongoing spoken ride
information; alarm usage is documented for alarms. Alarm routing selects a volume/
policy category, not an acoustic boost. Automatic shared-volume adjustment and
restoration are best-effort, including process-death/manual-change observation
limits already recorded in alarm-volume report. 80% is a chosen default, not a
measured loudness calibration. Recommend an explicit alarm/media route choice for
a general app and retain Pixel verification before treating this as validated.
No implementation changes or new tests; review only. Next: Pixel listening and
volume/focus/routing checks, then decide whether route selection is useful.
